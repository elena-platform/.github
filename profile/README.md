# ELENA AI

**Private AI infrastructure for organizations handling sensitive business data.**

ELENA is a private-first AI platform designed for legal, advisory, financial, and other knowledge-intensive organizations that require control, auditability, and flexible deployment.

It combines document intelligence, conversational AI, and secure workflow automation inside environments where data exposure is not acceptable by default.

---

## What ELENA Does

### Document Intelligence
Private search, retrieval, and question answering across internal documents and knowledge bases.

### Conversational AI
Natural language interaction designed for internal business workflows and high-trust environments.

### Workflow Automation
Automation for knowledge-intensive tasks with human oversight, access control, and operational traceability.

### Auditability
Full audit logging of AI interactions and operational events, built for accountability and compliance-oriented environments.

### Infrastructure Control
Deploy in isolated cloud environments, on dedicated private infrastructure, or directly on-premise.

---

## Deployment Models

### Managed Private Cloud
ELENA-managed deployment with isolated tenant environments and controlled infrastructure boundaries.

### Customer-Controlled Private Deployment
Deployment on customer-owned servers or dedicated private cloud infrastructure.

### Air-Gapped Environments *(Roadmap)*
Designed for organizations requiring maximum isolation and zero external connectivity.

---

## Built For

ELENA is designed for organizations that cannot treat AI as a generic public SaaS layer.

Typical use cases include:

- Legal and advisory firms
- Financial and regulated businesses
- Knowledge-intensive SMEs
- Teams operating under confidentiality, compliance, or data residency constraints

---

## Platform Principles

ELENA is built around a set of practical principles:

- **Private by default**
- **Deployment flexibility**
- **Data isolation**
- **Operational auditability**
- **Human oversight**
- **Enterprise-oriented control**

We believe organizations adopting AI in sensitive environments need more than model access. They need infrastructure, governance, and deployment models that reflect real operational constraints.
